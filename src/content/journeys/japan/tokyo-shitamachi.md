<div class="bilingual-block">
  <p class="en-prose">
    Late November in Tokyo strips the urban landscape down to clean geometry. Walking into the University of Tokyo Hongo campus, the Gothic red-brick arcades keep the low morning sun firmly outside the corridors, casting deep, crisp shadows between the pillars. In the central courtyard, a massive ginkgo stands in saturated yellow. Standing near the bottom stone step, the dark architectural lines balance the dense yellow foliage above.
  </p>
  <p class="zh-prose">
    东京十一月下旬的黄叶期来得利落。走入东京大学本乡校区，法文馆两侧的哥特式红砖拱廊把直射光压在回廊外侧，廊柱间的阴影很实。中央的大银杏树挂满正黄色的叶片，晨光从建筑尖顶斜切下来，把树冠和地面的落叶照成一大片明黄。贴在回廊台阶边缘取景，暗色砖石的纵深感刚好把饱满的树冠衬托在上方。
  </p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-block">
  <p class="en-prose">
    The night before around Asakusa, the temperature dropped quickly after sunset. The crowds in front of Senso-ji thinned out, leaving the vermilion eaves of Hozomon gate standing sharp under floodlights. Shooting handheld in the chill, the camera pulled the texture of dark timber and damp stone paving out of the shadows, with faint amber reflections spilling from shuttered street stalls.
  </p>
  <p class="zh-prose">
    前一晚在浅草寺，夜里的温度降得很快。宝藏门前游人散去，朱红色的重檐在泛光灯下立得很直。没有三脚架，手持在寒风里按快门，暗处的木料纹理和石板路原样收了进来。路面带着白天的薄潮气，反着两侧摊位微弱的暖光。
  </p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-block">
  <p class="en-prose">
    By midday, the route led to Hijiribashi bridge in Ochanomizu. Looking east over the Kanda River, commuter lines crisscross across multiple tiers of steel and concrete. Waiting along the pedestrian parapet for twenty minutes, the orange Chuo line, yellow Sobu line, and red Marunouchi subway cars intersected cleanly beneath the bridge arches, a momentary lock of urban tempo against the dark green river below.
  </p>
  <p class="zh-prose">
    中午走到御茶之水圣桥。站在桥栏向东看神田川，几层铁轨在混凝土桥孔间错开。在桥头等了二十分钟，中央线、总武线与丸之内线列车在不同高度交错穿过。钢架的深灰与河面的浑绿叠在一起，截下列车交汇的瞬间，这是城市节奏最清晰的切面。
  </p>
</div>
