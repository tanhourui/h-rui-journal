<div class="bilingual-block">
  <p class="en-prose">
    Kyoto in October carries a sense of timeless balance. Walking up the gentle stone incline toward Hokan-ji, the five-story Yasaka Pagoda anchors the entire perspective, flanked by dark cedar facades and gently curved roof tiles. In the afternoon light, groups in traditional yukata move slowly up the slopes, their colorful sashes adding vivid accents against the weathered timber lattices.
  </p>
  <p class="zh-prose">
    十月的京都自带一种沉静的平衡感。沿着东山的青石板缓步向上，法观寺八坂塔的五重飞檐稳稳立在坡道尽头，两侧是深色的连片町家与低垂的瓦檐。午后的阳光斜照在石阶上，身着和服的人们缓步而行，腰间艳丽的织物在灰褐色的旧木格窗前格外醒目。
  </p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-block">
  <p class="en-prose">
    Before the morning tour buses arrived, Ninenzaka lay entirely empty at six in the morning. A single stone lantern outside a closed tea shop remained illuminated, its warm yellow bulb reflecting off damp cobblestones. At Kinkaku-ji, the morning sun struck the gold leaf of the upper pavilion with pristine intensity, casting a perfect inverted double onto the dark, still waters of Mirror Pond.
  </p>
  <p class="zh-prose">
    清晨六点赶在游人到来之前探访二年坂，整条石坡空无一人。闭门的茶寮檐下留着一盏孤灯，昏黄的灯火映在微润的石板路上，泛出幽微的光泽。转至金阁寺，初升的阳光径直打在上层耀眼的金箔上，无风的水面犹如明镜，将整座楼阁的金色倒影完完整整地收录其中。
  </p>
</div>
