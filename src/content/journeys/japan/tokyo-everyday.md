<div class="bilingual-block">
  <p class="en-prose">
    Walking up to the KITTE rooftop garden in Marunouchi just as dusk settles, Tokyo Station unfolds below like an intricate architectural model. Floodlights illuminate the red brick facade, ornamental gables, and copper domes, while the evening commute swirls through the stone plaza. Resting the camera against the glass railing to stay steady, a slow shutter blurs the passing taxi taillights into fine streaks of gold and red, contrasting with the solid heritage brickwork.
  </p>
  <p class="zh-prose">
    傍晚走上丸之内 KITTE 顶楼露台，天色刚刚转暗。东京站丸之内侧的红砖主建筑在站前广场正前方舒展铺开，地灯打在石雕山花与青铜圆顶上。端着相机靠在玻璃栏杆边缘避开反光，慢快门把广场上穿行出租车的尾灯拉成细长光轨，与红砖墙体的厚重纹理形成对比。
  </p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-block">
  <p class="en-prose">
    The final morning led through quiet residential paths. In neighborhood parks, the city slows down. Next to a wooden pavilion shelter, an electric child-seat mamachari bicycle sat parked directly beneath a blazing crimson maple tree. A few steps away, a single fallen leaf rested on wet dark stone, lit by a lone shaft of morning sunlight filtering through the canopy.
  </p>
  <p class="zh-prose">
    旅途的最后一天走回居民区附近的社区公园。东京的生活节奏在这些角落慢下来。公园凉亭边停着一辆装了儿童座椅的电动自行车，正上方是一整株红得发亮的枫树。几米外的步道还留着薄水渍，一片刚落下的红叶贴在深色湿石面上，恰好被一小束漏下来的晨光罩住，清清楚楚。
  </p>
</div>
