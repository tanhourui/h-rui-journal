<div class="bilingual-block">
  <p class="en-prose">
    Late November transforms Tokyo into an exhibition of pure yellow. At the University of Tokyo Hongo campus, century-old ginkgo trees form an unbroken vault overhead, filtering the bright autumn sunlight into a warm, diffused haze. Fallen leaves cover the stone flags like a thick golden carpet, while students and visitors stroll slowly beneath the dark Gothic brick facades.
  </p>
  <p class="zh-prose">
    十一月终了的东京把所有色彩让位给正黄。在东京大学本乡校区，百年树龄的银杏在头顶连成巨大的穹顶，将直射阳光过滤成一片柔和的金芒。石板路上积着厚厚的一层碎叶，踩上去沙沙作响，深褐色的哥特式红砖墙与明黄的叶片形成了极为利落的明暗对比。
  </p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-block">
  <p class="en-prose">
    A short train ride away in the older neighborhoods of Yanaka, the pace shifts from monumental boulevards to intimate alley life. Along the residential side streets, traditional timber storefronts like Yoshikawa display hand-carved wooden signboards and rows of potted houseplants on simple wooden benches. It is Tokyo in its most tender, unhurried form: quiet, weathered, and deeply rooted in domestic routine.
  </p>
  <p class="zh-prose">
    乘车转入谷中一带，视野从宏大的行道树转入充满烟火气的老街。在谷中银座周边的里巷，像よし川这样的传统小店依旧保留着手刻木招牌、波浪铁皮雨棚与一排排盛在白色花盆里的小绿植。这里没有银座的霓虹闪烁，只有日式下町特有的生活底色，安静、质朴，带着年深日久的温度。
  </p>
</div>
