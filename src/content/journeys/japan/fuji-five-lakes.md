<div class="bilingual-block">
  <p class="en-prose">
    We arrived at the shoreline of Lake Yamanaka at twenty past five in the morning. A crust of white frost covered the wooden boardwalk, cracking softly under my boots. The air hovered right around freezing with almost no wind. High above the snow-covered crater of Mount Fuji, a dense lenticular cap cloud held its rigid shape against the cold dawn blue for nearly thirty minutes. The shoreline railing served as a natural guide, leading out toward the calm lake and the volcanic peak.
  </p>
  <p class="zh-prose">
    清晨五点二十分抵达山中湖畔，岸边木栈道结了一层硬白霜，踩上去咯吱作响。气温在零度上下，风很轻。火山口正上方悬着一团厚实的笠云，轮廓紧绷，在黎明冷蓝的晨光里静止了将近半小时。贴着木栏杆取景，湖面平静，远处的富士山稳实地压在视线正中。
  </p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-block">
  <p class="en-prose">
    Right around sunrise, the water was noticeably warmer than the frigid air, sending curls of white steam rolling across the surface. A family of mute swans cruised quietly through the golden shallows. Just before six o'clock, one swan turned into the rising sun and suddenly beat its wings against the lake, scattering beads of golden spray across the water while the morning steam drifted in the background.
  </p>
  <p class="zh-prose">
    太阳刚出头的那几分钟，湖水温度明显高过冷空气，水面腾起一层层白汽。几只大天鹅贴着浅水游动。六点前，一只天鹅突然迎着朝阳逆光拍打双翼，羽毛边缘激起的水珠在阳光下粒粒分明，飞溅的水花与蒸腾的薄雾同时定格在画面里。
  </p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-block">
  <p class="en-prose">
    By afternoon, the journey crossed over to the Momiji Corridor in Kawaguchiko. The mood shifted entirely from the morning silence. Along both banks of the stone water channel, Japanese maples formed a dense glowing canopy over the pedestrian footbridge. Warm autumn sunlight filtered through the crimson branches, bathing the cobblestones and visitors below in deep scarlet.
  </p>
  <p class="zh-prose">
    午后转道河口湖红叶回廊。这边的热闹和山中湖清晨完全两样。小溪两岸的枫树全红了，枝条在头顶搭成一道拱顶。站在木质步行桥前，桥上观赏的人群与头顶密集的红叶重叠在一起。阳光穿透层层红叶落下来，整条石砌水渠都被映成了深红色。
  </p>
</div>
