    Dawn at Lake Kawaguchiko arrives with a crisp chill. Along the northern shoreline near Oishi Park, tall silver susuki grasses bow gently to the breeze, their plumes catching the first amber glow of sunrise. Directly across the calm water, Mount Fuji stands monumental, its rugged ridges etched sharply against a pale morning sky.
