<div class="bilingual-block">
  <p class="en-prose">
    Standing on the thirty-third floor observation deck at Azabudai Hills in the morning, Tokyo unrolls in layered density toward the bay. Tokyo Tower rises immediately in front, its orange steel lattice catching clean northern light against the glassy expanse of high-rises. Moving west toward Meiji Jingu Gaien, the famous ginkgo avenue reaches peak autumn maturity. Taxis and a blue sports car roll steadily through fallen yellow leaves under the dense canopy arch.
  </p>
  <p class="zh-prose">
    上午登上麻布台之丘三十三楼观景台，东京城区的稠密向海湾方向铺开。东京塔立在眼前，橙白相间的钢架迎着清亮的光线，背后是高层建筑群的玻璃立面。随后转去明治神宫外苑，著名的银杏大道叶片正浓。出租车与一辆亮蓝色跑车在密集的黄叶拱顶下穿行，落叶铺满了路侧。
  </p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-block">
  <p class="en-prose">
    After walking the sunny lawns of Shinjuku Gyoen, the afternoon drew down toward Tokyo Bay at Odaiba. As the sun slid toward the western horizon, the entire bay ignited in copper and orange. From the waterfront promenade, looking past the industrial container cranes of the shipping port, the clean conical silhouette of Mount Fuji appeared etched against the fiery twilight sky, a tranquil volcanic outline presiding over the metallic pulse of the harbor.
  </p>
  <p class="zh-prose">
    穿过新宿御苑洒满阳光的草坪，下午顺着轨道前往台场海滨。太阳滑向西面地平线，整片东京湾被照成厚重的铜橙色。站在海滨步道向远方望去，集装箱装卸龙门吊的铁架剪影整齐排开。远处的富士山尖锥形轮廓在火红的晚霞里清清楚楚显出来，安静地立在繁忙的海港天际线之后。
  </p>
