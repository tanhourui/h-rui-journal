<div class="bilingual-block">
  <p class="en-prose">
    Arriving over the Gulf of Thailand, Bangkok opens as a vast sprawl bisected by the murky ribbon of the Chao Phraya River. In Chinatown’s Yaowarat corridor, night falls under a blaze of vertical neon shop signs. Searing oil, boiling broth from Guay Jub Ouan Pochana, and the whine of passing Tuk-Tuks fill the humid air as pedestrians pack the narrow curbside. Across the river, the white porcelain mosaics of Wat Arun and its towering Yaksha guardians stand firm under the tropical sun.
  </p>
  <p class="zh-prose">
    飞机掠过泰国湾上空，曼谷以湄南河为中轴向四方延展。唐人街耀华力路的夜晚被层层叠叠的霓虹金字招牌点亮。油锅的翻滚声、陈亿粿条店弥漫的胡椒肉汤香气与突突车的轰鸣交织在闷热的夜风中。隔河相望，郑王庙洁白的瓷片塔尖与门前驻守的夜叉巨像在烈日下巍然伫立。
  </p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-block">
  <p class="en-prose">
    An hour north, the ancient capital of Ayutthaya reveals a quieter weight of time. Weathered red-brick stupas, the serene stone Buddha face cradled by banyan roots at Wat Mahathat, and the stark white terraces of Wat Phu Khao Thong rise above the plains. Further along, the 42-meter stone reclining Buddha of Wat Lokayasutharam rests beneath the open sky, wrapped in saffron cloth against centuries of sun and rain.
  </p>
  <p class="zh-prose">
    往北一小时车程，大城府的古遗迹呈现出全然不同的岁月厚度。风化斑驳的红砖佛塔、玛哈泰寺被菩提树根紧紧缠绕的安详佛首，以及普考同寺拔地而起的纯白折角台阶静立于平原之上。罗卡雅苏塔寺长达42米的巨型卧佛披着金黄袈裟露天仰卧，在漫长光阴与风雨中凝望苍穹。
  </p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-block">
  <p class="en-prose">
    April brings Songkran, and Bangkok shifts into an electric celebration. At the S2O Festival outside Rajamangala Stadium, high-pressure water cannons drench thousands of revelers while multi-colored lasers pierce curtains of falling water. Drenched crowds, glowing wristbands, and thumping bass turn the city’s water traditions into an overwhelming sensory wave before the journey settles into quiet sunset reflections along the Chao Phraya.
  </p>
  <p class="zh-prose">
    四月的泼水节将曼谷推向电光与水流的狂欢。在拉加曼加拉体育场旁的S2O音乐节现场，高压水炮向数万人群喷射出漫天水雾，五彩激光刺破翻卷的水帘。浑身湿透的年轻人在强劲重低音与火焰特效中尽情跃动，将传统的祈福水花化作现代电音的感官浪潮，最终在湄南河畔温柔的金黄余晖中缓缓归于宁静。
  </p>
</div>
