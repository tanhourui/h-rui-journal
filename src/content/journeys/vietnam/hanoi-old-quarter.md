<div class="bilingual-block">
  <p class="en-prose">
    Hanoi operates at the intersection of mechanical raw power and colonial stillness. At Train Street, residential life retreats by mere inches as the red diesel locomotive Đ 12 E-628 rumbles past dining tables and laundry racks. Eastward over the Red River, the century-old cantilever trusses of Long Bien Bridge stand oxidized in deep umber, carrying motorbikes and trains along its riveted iron skeleton just as it did a century ago.
  </p>
  <p class="zh-prose">
    河内的日常在机械的轰鸣与法式殖民建筑的沉静之间交错上演。在火车街狭窄的居民弄堂里，红色柴油机车Đ 12 E-628擦着街边的餐桌与门槛呼啸而过，铁轨两旁的市井生活在火车驶过的前后几秒内收放自如。向东横跨红河的龙编桥，锈蚀沉郁的桁架横梁承载着呼啸而过的摩托车与货运列车，如同一座活着的钢铁史诗。
  </p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-block">
  <p class="en-prose">
    Escaping the capital into the northern highlands of Sapa, alpine mists envelope the yellow clocktower of Sun Plaza and the stone bell tower of the Catholic Church. Below, the red carriages of the Muong Hoa funicular cross arched steel viaducts suspended high above the terraced valley, while the cliffside glass lift at Fansipan disappears into white mountain clouds. Returning to Hanoi, Sunday worshippers on red plastic stools fill the courtyard of St. Joseph Cathedral, before evening settles into the warm amber candlelight of The Alchemist and the golden sunset across West Lake.
  </p>
  <p class="zh-prose">
    一路向北驶入沙坝的云雾高原，山谷间的湿冷雾气笼罩着太阳广场明黄色的法式钟楼与石教堂顶端的幽蓝十字架。红色的芒花山谷缆车高高穿行在峡谷之上的铁架桥梁间，番西邦悬崖电梯在漫天云海中若隐若现。回到首都，数百名信众坐在红色塑料矮凳上齐聚大教堂广场聆听主日弥撒；当夜幕降临，炼金术士酒吧的琥珀烛光与西湖边温柔的金色落日，为这趟越南之行画上宁静的句点。
  </p>
</div>
