<div class="bilingual-block">
  <p class="en-prose">
    Hanoi mixes mechanical roar with the quiet of old buildings. In the narrow residential alleys of Train Street, red diesel locomotives scrape past dining tables and doorways, while locals seamlessly resume their routines seconds after the train departs. Eastward across the Red River, the rusted trusses of Long Bien Bridge still carry passing motorbikes and freight trains, unchanged for over a century.
  </p>
  <p class="zh-prose">
    河内总是混合着机械轰鸣与旧建筑的沉静。在火车街狭窄的居民弄堂里，红色柴油机车擦着街边的餐桌与门槛开过，人们在列车驶离后立刻恢复了日常。向东横跨红河的龙编桥，锈蚀的桁架横梁依然承载着来往的摩托车与货运列车，百年来没有什么改变。
  </p>
</div>

<div class="bilingual-block">
  <p class="en-prose">
    Heading north into the Sapa highlands, cold mist swallows the yellow French clocktower and the blue neon cross atop the stone church. Red valley trains cross iron viaducts spanning the gorge, while elevators disappear into seas of cloud. Back in the capital, churchgoers sit on red plastic stools gathered in the cathedral plaza; as night falls, the amber candlelight of a speakeasy and the sunset over West Lake form the final memories of this trip.
  </p>
  <p class="zh-prose">
    向北进入沙坝的高原，湿冷的雾气笼罩着明黄色的法式钟楼与石教堂顶端的幽蓝十字架。红色的山谷列车穿行在峡谷上的铁架桥梁间，电梯隐入漫天云海。回到首都，信众坐在红色塑料矮凳上齐聚大教堂广场；入夜后，酒吧的琥珀烛光与西湖的落日，构成了这趟旅程最后的记忆。
  </p>
</div>
